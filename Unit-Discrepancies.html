Unit & material-link discrepancies

BOQ lines, purchase-order lines and materials are linked by name rather than by ID, and each carries its own free-text unit. Two things can go wrong silently: a name that matches nothing, so consumption is never recorded; or two units that measure different things, so the quantity is deducted in the wrong unit. Neither raises an error on its own. This is every instance, grouped by what to do about it.
